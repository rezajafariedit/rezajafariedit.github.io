05 / A few details

Before we start.

Anything else, ask me on LinkedIn.

What kinds of videos do you edit?

Short-form for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts; podcast episodes and clips; brand commercials and product films; true-crime and documentary long-form for YouTube; and fully AI-generated videos.

Can I judge the work before committing?

Yes. New projects start with a free sample edit on your own footage.

What do you need from me?

Raw footage by Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer. Scripts, brand assets and reference videos help but aren't required. Structuring rough material is my job.

How do revisions work?

You get a review cut, send timestamped notes, and I refine from there. Long-form goes through the same multi-round QC I run with YouTube production teams.

Will AI make my video look artificial?

No. I use Kling, Seedance, Runway, Higgsfield, Midjourney and Nano Banana for scenes and characters, ElevenLabs and Sync.so for voice, and finish everything by hand in Premiere Pro, After Effects and DaVinci Resolve.

Which languages and time zones do you cover?

English and Persian content, remotely from Istanbul for clients worldwide, including US teams on their own schedules.
